Weeknight Lentil Soup

Ready in 40 minutes and it freezes well.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown lentils
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 6 cups vegetable stock

Method

  1. Soften the onion and carrots in olive oil for eight minutes.
  2. Add the lentils and stock, then simmer for 25 minutes.
  3. Season with lemon juice and salt before serving.
