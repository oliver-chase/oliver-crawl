City Council Approves Bike Lane Plan

The council voted 7 to 2 on Tuesday to build 18 miles of protected bike lanes by 2028.

Construction begins on Harbor Street this autumn, funded by a state transportation grant of 12 million dollars.

Opponents raised concerns about parking, and the plan now includes 300 new spaces in nearby garages.
