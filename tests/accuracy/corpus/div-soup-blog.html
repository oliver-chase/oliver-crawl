Notes on Sourdough Hydration

Hydration is the ratio of water to flour by weight. A seventy percent dough feels tacky but holds its shape on the counter.

Whole wheat flour drinks more water, so add it in small amounts and wait two minutes before judging the dough.

Wetter doughs open the crumb but spread when shaped, which is why many bakers bake them in a dutch oven for support.

Keep a log of flour, water and room temperature each bake so the next loaf improves on the last one.
