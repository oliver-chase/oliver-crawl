Retry policy

The Widget API retries failed webhook deliveries up to five times with exponential backoff.

Backoff schedule

The first retry happens after 30 seconds, and each later retry doubles the wait.

POST /v1/webhooks/retry
{"max_attempts": 5}

After the fifth failure the endpoint is marked unhealthy and deliveries pause.
