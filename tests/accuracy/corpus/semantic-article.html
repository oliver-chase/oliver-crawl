Why Salt Marshes Matter

By Dana Whitfield, March 3

Salt marshes absorb storm surge before it reaches inland towns, and a single hectare can store more carbon than a hectare of tropical forest.

Nursery for fish

Juvenile herring and flounder shelter in the tidal creeks until they are large enough to move offshore.

What is changing

Sea level rise squeezes marshes against sea walls, so restoration projects now plan room for them to migrate inland.
