Harbor Ferry Timetable

The ferry crosses to the island four times a day between April and October.

Departures: 8:00, 11:30, 15:00 and 18:30 from the main pier. Journey time is 25 minutes.

Foot passengers pay 6 dollars, and bicycles travel free.
